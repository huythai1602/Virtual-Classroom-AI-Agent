Columns in quizzes:
- id (integer)
- lesson_id (integer)
- questionCount (integer)
- required_progress (integer)
- title (character varying)
- description (text)

Columns in lessons:
- id (integer)
- created_at (timestamp without time zone)
- updated_at (timestamp without time zone)
- course_id (integer)
- order (integer)
- duration (integer)
- status (USER-DEFINED)
- content_type (character varying)
- title (character varying)
- content (text)
- content_key (character varying)
